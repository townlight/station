This is an additional permission under section 7 of the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 (AGPL-3.0-only), for TownLight code whose copyright holders have expressly granted this exception (the **Covered Code**).

## 1. Eligible public institution

An **Eligible Institution** is (a) a government, governmental department, public authority, or special district; (b) a public school, public college or university, or public library; or (c) a nonprofit organization recognized under U.S. Internal Revenue Code section 501(c)(3), or an equivalent public-benefit charitable organization under the law of its home country, acting for its own charitable or public-benefit purposes.

## 2. Additional permission for its own deployment

An Eligible Institution may modify and operate Covered Code for its own governmental, educational, or charitable services, including websites accessible to the public, **without complying with section 13's requirement to offer the Corresponding Source of that modified version to remote users**.

An institution may use a contractor to run its deployment under this permission **only if** the contractor acts on the institution's behalf and under its direction and control, and a written agreement entitles the institution to obtain, use, modify, and have a replacement provider operate the Corresponding Source of modifications made by that contractor for the deployment. This permission does not cover the contractor's independently operated, general-purpose or multi-customer commercial software service. Such a service may still be offered under the ordinary AGPL.

## 3. No change to other AGPL obligations

This exception relaxes **only section 13** and only for Covered Code within a qualifying deployment. It does not change AGPL requirements for distributing copies, grant trademark rights, or override any third-party component's license. It does not limit anyone's rights under the AGPL, including commercial rights.

Under section 7 of the AGPL, a person conveying a copy may remove this additional permission from that copy. The AGPL's warranty and liability provisions remain applicable.
