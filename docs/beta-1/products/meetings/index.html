Townlight Meetings · Townlight Beta 1

Keep the agenda, decisions and evidence connected.

Meetings supports clerk preparation and the retained proceedings of a meeting: agenda items, packets, motions, votes, supplied transcript material, minutes decisions and archives. It supplies canonical meeting and agenda context to Station.

For Clerks, meeting coordinators and authorized reviewers

What the workflow delivers

  1. Create the meeting. Enter its title, date/time including the intended time zone, location and meeting type. Save and reopen it before adding downstream evidence.
  2. Prepare the agenda and packet. Create agenda items, assign them to the actual meeting and record review. Assemble a packet from the selected items and source documents. Check the saved assembly’s packet identifier before using it for an archive or delivery.
  3. Record notice work. Use the existing notice checklist, template and posting-proof controls where applicable. A calculated checklist is evidence for clerk review; staff verify the jurisdiction’s notice requirements and actual posting.
  4. Record proceedings. Capture the actual motion, seconder and votes. Record corrections as corrections, preserving the earlier event. Retain supplied transcript text with its source label.

Connected evidence

Provides evidence to Records, Code, Zoning and Planning, and meeting identity to Station. Code adoption and codification remain separate. Supporting Notice and Access do not replace clerk review.

Know the beta boundary

Supplied transcript handling is not automatic audio transcription. Station adds capture; it does not imply a Meetings livestream hosting service. Vendor sync code and adapters are not proof that every vendor is configured or live.
