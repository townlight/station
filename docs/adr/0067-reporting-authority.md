# ADR 0067: Reporting authority

## Decision

`station-reporting` owns a pure, fail-closed contract for consuming typed as-run
facts, projecting them over exact UTC windows, deriving deterministic hours by
franchise/category, configuring EPG export forms, validating X-List/XMLTV/CSV renderer
input, and recording immutable export receipts. It does not read, mutate, or replace
the worker journal.

An as-run projection names one channel, revision, exact UTC half-open window, and
ordered fact-provenance hashes. Its facts must be unique, sequence-contiguous, and
time-contiguous from the exact window start through end; sequence gaps, duplicates,
time gaps/overlaps, channel mismatch, and substituted provenance fail closed. Hours
are not accepted as a caller total: the authority deterministically aggregates exact
fact duration by ordered franchise/category keys and validates reports against that
result and the exact source hashes.

X-List, XMLTV, and CSV contracts carry typed rows that exactly reproduce source facts.
Text is bounded and rejects controls, markup, CSV separators, backslashes, and
spreadsheet-formula prefixes. EPG configuration has revision-one creation and CAS
replacement; its identity/channel cannot be rebound. Export receipts are append-only
and one-per-export, with outcomes that describe only future rendering acceptance or
rejection—not generated bytes or delivery.

## Boundaries and non-claims

This crate has no worker-journal reader/writer, persistence, API, UI, EPG feed
renderer, filesystem/network export delivery, scheduler, timezone conversion runtime,
or installed/field proof. It does not establish that as-run facts are truthful, that a
report/export was rendered or delivered, that a receiver consumed it, or that an EPG
listing was published. Later slices must obtain facts from the authoritative journal,
persist projections/configurations/receipts through the single SQLite writer, render
and safely deliver exports, and acquire installed-machine and field evidence.
